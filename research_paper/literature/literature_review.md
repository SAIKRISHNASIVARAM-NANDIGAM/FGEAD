# Literature Review — 20 Verified Research Papers
## FGEAD: Feature-level Graph-based Explainable Anomaly Detection

| No. | Category | Paper | Year | Method | Domain/Dataset | XAI Method | Main Finding | Limitation | Relevance to Our Work |
|-----|----------|-------|------|--------|----------------|------------|-------------|------------|----------------------|
| 1 | Explainable AI | Barredo Arrieta et al. — "Explainable Artificial Intelligence (XAI): Concepts, taxonomies, opportunities and challenges toward responsible AI" | 2020 | Survey / Taxonomy | General AI | Taxonomy of XAI methods | Comprehensive classification of XAI into transparent models and post-hoc techniques; establishes responsible AI framework | Broad survey; no specific time-series implementation | Foundational taxonomy for positioning FGEAD's custom post-hoc explainability approach |
| 2 | Explainable AI | Ribeiro et al. — "Why Should I Trust You?: Explaining the Predictions of Any Classifier" | 2016 | LIME — Local surrogate models | General classification | Local interpretable explanations | Model-agnostic local explanations via perturbation-based linear approximation | Linear approximation fails to capture temporal and inter-metric dynamics in time series | Benchmark contrast: FGEAD uses deviation-based and graph-relationship XAI instead of LIME |
| 3 | Explainable AI | Lundberg and Lee — "A Unified Approach to Interpreting Model Predictions" | 2017 | SHAP — Shapley values | General ML | Additive feature attribution | Unifies feature attribution methods under game-theoretic Shapley framework | Computationally expensive; assumes feature independence violated by correlated time series | Benchmark contrast: FGEAD avoids SHAP's independence assumption by using graph-aware deviation analysis |
| 4 | Explainable AI | Rudin — "Stop explaining black box machine learning models for high stakes decisions and use interpretable models instead" | 2019 | Interpretable model design | High-stakes decisions | Inherent interpretability | Argues post-hoc explanations can be misleading; advocates inherently interpretable architectures | Fully interpretable models may sacrifice capacity for complex anomaly detection | Supports FGEAD's design choice of semi-interpretable architecture where attention graph provides structural interpretability |
| 5 | Explainable AI | Koh and Liang — "Understanding Black-box Predictions via Influence Functions" | 2017 | Influence functions | Deep learning | Training-data attribution | Traces predictions back to influential training instances using robust statistics | Computationally prohibitive for large datasets; numerically unstable | Justifies FGEAD's focus on feature-level and relationship-level explanations rather than instance-level influence |
| 6 | Explainable Anomaly Detection | Zhao et al. — "Multivariate Time-series Anomaly Detection via Graph Attention Network" (MTAD-GAT) | 2020 | GAT + forecasting + reconstruction | MTS / SMD, SMAP, MSL | Attention weights as feature importance | Joint temporal-spatial modeling with graph attention captures inter-metric correlations | Explainability is a byproduct of attention, not a dedicated post-hoc framework | Closest architectural baseline; FGEAD extends with explicit deviation analysis and structured explanation generation |
| 7 | Explainable Anomaly Detection | Li et al. — "Multivariate Time Series Anomaly Detection and Interpretation using Hierarchical Inter-Metric and Temporal Embedding" (InterFusion) | 2021 | Hierarchical VAE | MTS / SMD, SMAP, MSL | MCMC-based metric localization | Captures inter-metric and temporal dependencies hierarchically; MCMC interpretation identifies anomalous metrics | MCMC interpretation is slow for real-time root-cause analysis | Shares FGEAD's goal of feature-level root-cause analysis; FGEAD achieves this faster via direct deviation computation |
| 8 | Explainable Anomaly Detection | Deng and Hooi — "Graph Neural Network-Based Anomaly Detection in Multivariate Time Series" (GDN) | 2021 | GNN + graph structure learning | MTS / SWaT, WADI | Learned graph structure | Infers directed feature dependency graph; uses deviation from learned relationships to detect anomalies | Focuses on detection accuracy; lacks structured human-readable explanatory output | Deeply aligned with FGEAD's core architecture; FGEAD adds the structured 5-question explanation framework |
| 9 | Explainable Anomaly Detection | Dai and Chen — "Graph-Augmented Normalizing Flows for Anomaly Detection of Multiple Time Series" (GANF) | 2022 | Normalizing flows + Bayesian graph | MTS / SWaT, WADI | Causal graph discovery | Discovers directed causal graph using Bayesian network; detects anomalies via density estimation | Requires massive continuous data; difficult to adapt to shifting server profiles | Graph discovery concept parallels FGEAD's graph relationship auditing |
| 10 | Explainable Anomaly Detection | Jacob et al. — "Exathlon: A Benchmark for Explainable Anomaly Detection over Time Series" | 2021 | Benchmark framework | Time series benchmark | Root-cause localization metrics | First benchmark for evaluating XAI quality in time-series anomaly detection | Limited to specific synthetic/trace datasets; metrics may not transfer to SMD | Provides evaluation methodology for assessing FGEAD's explanation quality |
| 11 | Explainable Anomaly Detection | Tripathy et al. — "Explaining Anomalies in Industrial Multivariate Time-series Data with the help of eXplainable AI" | 2022 | Autoencoder + SHAP/LIME | Industrial MTS | SHAP, LIME post-hoc | Applies standard XAI techniques to autoencoder-based anomaly detection for industrial root-cause analysis | Relies on generic post-hoc tools; does not leverage graph network topology for explanation | Reinforces FGEAD's objective of moving beyond standard SHAP/LIME toward graph-based domain-aware explanations |
| 12 | Anomaly Detection / ML | Su et al. — "OmniAnomaly: Robust Anomaly Detection for Multivariate Time Series through Stochastic Recurrent Neural Network" | 2019 | Stochastic RNN + normalizing flows | MTS / SMD (introduces dataset) | Reconstruction probability | Introduces SMD dataset; models normal patterns via stochastic recurrent networks; proposes metric-level interpretation | Processes features independently; fails to model inter-feature graph topology | Origin of the SMD dataset used by FGEAD; foundational reconstruction-based benchmark |
| 13 | Anomaly Detection / ML | Audibert et al. — "USAD: UnSupervised Anomaly Detection on Multivariate Time Series" | 2020 | Adversarial autoencoders | MTS / SMD, SWaT | None | Adversarial training amplifies anomaly reconstruction error; achieves fast training | No temporal sequence layers (LSTM); limited temporal dependency modeling | Non-graph deep learning baseline for comparison with FGEAD's graph+LSTM approach |
| 14 | Anomaly Detection / ML | Zhang et al. — "A Deep Neural Network for Unsupervised Anomaly Detection and Diagnosis in Multivariate Time Series Data" (MSCRED) | 2019 | ConvLSTM + signature matrices | MTS / synthetic, power plant | Inter-sensor correlation matrices | Constructs multi-scale signature matrices capturing inter-sensor correlations for root-cause diagnosis | Dense matrices scale quadratically with features; inefficient for high-dimensional data | Conceptually similar to FGEAD's graph adjacency approach; FGEAD uses sparse attention instead of dense correlation matrices |
| 15 | Anomaly Detection / ML | Xu et al. — "Anomaly Transformer: Time Series Anomaly Detection with Association Discrepancy" | 2022 | Transformer + association discrepancy | MTS / SMD, MSL, SMAP, SWaT | Attention discrepancy | Uses prior-association vs. series-association divergence in self-attention to detect anomalies | Purely temporal attention; does not map or explain feature-to-feature causal relationships | Parallels FGEAD's use of attention deviation; FGEAD extends to explicit inter-feature graph explanation |
| 16 | GNN & Feature Attribution | Ying et al. — "GNNExplainer: Generating Explanations for Graph Neural Networks" | 2019 | Optimization-based subgraph/feature mask | Graph classification / node classification | Compact subgraph + feature subset | First model-agnostic GNN explanation method; identifies crucial subgraph structures and features | Requires per-instance optimization; computationally prohibitive for continuous time series streams | Theoretical basis for FGEAD's feature-level graph auditing; FGEAD avoids per-instance optimization |
| 17 | GNN & Feature Attribution | Luo et al. — "Parameterized Explainer for Graph Neural Network" (PGExplainer) | 2020 | Parameterized edge mask network | Graph classification | Learned edge masks | Trains a parameterized network to generate explanations efficiently, overcoming GNNExplainer's per-instance bottleneck | Explanation quality depends on parameterizing network; struggles with out-of-distribution anomalous subgraphs | FGEAD's direct deviation approach bypasses both per-instance optimization and parameterization |
| 18 | GNN & Feature Attribution | Jain and Wallace — "Attention is not Explanation" | 2019 | Attention analysis | NLP classification | Attention weights | Demonstrates that attention weights frequently do not correlate with gradient-based feature importance | Analysis primarily on NLP tasks; graph-structured attention may behave differently | Critical justification for FGEAD's design: attention graph alone is insufficient; dedicated post-hoc deviation analysis is necessary |
| 19 | Recent 2024–2026 | Lee et al. — "Explainable Time Series Anomaly Detection using Masked Latent Generative Modeling" | 2024 | Masked latent generative model | MTS | Feature-level latent patch reconstruction | Achieves robust detection with feature-level explanations via masked latent patch reconstruction | Generative reconstruction may hallucinate in noisy server environments | Modern non-graph contrast to FGEAD's forecasting-based graph approach |
| 20 | Recent 2024–2026 | Wang et al. — "A Survey of Deep Anomaly Detection in Multivariate Time Series: Taxonomy, Applications, and Directions" | 2025 | Survey | MTS / General | Survey of XAI trends | Comprehensive 2025 taxonomy confirming shift toward explainability and graph-based models | Survey only; no specific algorithmic solution | Validates FGEAD's research direction as current frontier of the field |
